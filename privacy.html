Gem Match – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Gem Match game ("the game") handles your information. In short: the game itself does not collect any personal data. If you use Google Play Games, Google stores your cloud save and achievements for you (see below).

Information the game collects

None. The game itself does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

Feedback emails

If you tap FEEDBACK in the game's settings (under MORE), your own email app opens with a message addressed to us. It already contains the game version, your level, your phone model, your Android version and your language, so we can understand any problem. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and you can change or delete any of it first. We only use feedback emails to answer you and improve the game, and we never share them.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, stars, settings and your chosen player name) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device unless you choose to copy a save code yourself, or use Google Play Games cloud save (see below). Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

In-app purchases

The game currently has nothing to buy. If optional purchases are added in a future update, they will be processed entirely by Google Play: the game will never see or store your payment details, and Google's handling of your payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games (cloud save and achievements)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game uses it to back up your game progress to your Play Games account (so it can be restored on a new phone) and to unlock Play Games achievements. This is handled by Google: your Play Games identity (gamer name and player ID), your saved game progress and your unlocked achievements are stored by Google, and Google's Play Games software also collects some diagnostic information. The game only reads and writes its own save and achievements, and never sees your email address. This is optional: if you don't use Google Play Games, the game works exactly the same, with your progress stored only on your device. You can delete your Play Games data at any time in your Play Games profile or your Google Account. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Reminders

If you choose to turn them on, the game can show an optional reminder (at most about once a day), for example when your daily reward is ready. These reminders are created and scheduled on your own device by the game itself. No data is sent anywhere to make them work. You can turn them off at any time in the game's settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Ratings

From time to time the game may show Google Play's own rating window, so you can rate the game without leaving it. This window is provided and handled entirely by Google Play; the game never sees whether or how you rated it. Google's handling of ratings and reviews is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The game uses these Android permissions, only on your device:

  • Vibration – haptic feedback during play (you can turn it off in the game's settings).
  • Notifications – only for the optional reminders described above, and only if you allow them.
  • Run at startup and keep awake – so a reminder you turned on still arrives after your phone restarts or while it is asleep.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
